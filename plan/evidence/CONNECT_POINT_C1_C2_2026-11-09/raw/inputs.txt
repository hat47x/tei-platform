  command: executor.Command (action id, kind, database/application)
  registry: map[core.CanonicalID]executor.Executor, supplied by the Host
  environment: read from the Workspace by routing.RouteCommand

  coordinator: *composition.Coordinator (Host generation pinning)
  runtime: pruntime.Registry (processes, verifier, service acceptance)
  routes: ServiceCarrierRouteSource (Host-owned, per pinned Binding)
  plan: pruntime.EnvironmentPlan (from the plugin lock + environment)
  selection: pruntime.ServiceProviderSelection (contract/scope/artifact)
