Attempt 1 is the most important result of this round.

It returned `ok`. The mapping was not in the World:

  persistenceMappings: 2   (only the pre-existing sales_core.order / order_line)

The loader reads only `databases/<db>/persistence/*.tei`. A `persist` file placed
anywhere else is not loaded, and validate says nothing about a file it did not
read.

A wrong placement therefore reports SUCCESS. `validate` passing does not mean the
intent landed. That is why every design round ends by reading the artefact back
through `world` or `inspect`.

"warn about an unloaded persist file" or "read a wider set of locations". Filing
from an unresolved fix is the mistake round 52 taught me to avoid.
