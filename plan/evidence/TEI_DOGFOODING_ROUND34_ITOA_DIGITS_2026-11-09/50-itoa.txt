itoa(4)   = "4"      itoa(10)  = ":"     <- punctuation
itoa(6)   = "6"      itoa(11)  = ";"
                     itoa(23)  = "G"
                     itoa(514) = "Ȳ"

The only caller is numberedFiles, which builds
    ^([0-9]{<itoa(digits)>})-.+\.md$
and is called with digits=6 (plan/issue) and digits=4 (plan/adr).
Both work today by accident: 4 and 6 are single digits.

At digits=10 the pattern becomes ^([0-9]{:})-.+\.md$ which
COMPILES (no panic) and matches nothing:
    matches 1234567890-x.md: false
    matches 1234-x.md:       false
So assertUnique would report "no duplicates" over an empty set.
The check does not fail; it stops existing.
