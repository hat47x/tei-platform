I diffed the canonical command list against `tei commands`:

  canonical: 56   discovered: 25   missing: 38

and every one of the 38 is accepted by the binary:

  $ tei scenario  -> scenario requires verify, generate, translate, run, or check
  $ tei artifacts -> exactly one of -workspace or -world is required

I was about to file this as a discoverability defect. It is not:

  Scope: "selected-read-only-commands"

and CLAUDE-facing design text says explicitly: "the list is NOT a complete
registry of all commands or plugin implementations", and a prior design note
already covers this exact case ("do not promote absence from the list into
'not implemented'").

The gap is declared, not accidental. No issue filed.
