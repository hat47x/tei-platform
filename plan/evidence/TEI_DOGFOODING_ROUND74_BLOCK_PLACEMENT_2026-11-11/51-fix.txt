expectEOF now checks, in order:
  1. a top-level declaration keyword   (existing misplacedDeclaration message)
  2. a block-only entry                (new)
  3. anything else                     (generic message)

Block-only entries: bind, resource, grant, lease-policy, deployment-executor,
observation-provider, evidence-provider, feature (environment); column,
primary-key, index (persist).

  "bind" cannot appear after the closing brace of its block; it belongs inside
  one, for example environment staging { ... ; bind <application>.<alias> ->
  <database-instance> }
