The legacy .tei validation body accepts exactly two words:

  case "attribute":
  case "relation":

But semantic.Validation also has Compared and GroupBy, and the semantic layer
requires them for no-overlap and not-after:

  SEMANTIC_VALIDATION_COMPARED_ATTRIBUTE_REQUIRED
    no-overlap semantic validation ... requires the attribute that ends the period

and nothing in the legacy parser ever sets them:

  $ grep -n 'v\.Compared\|v\.GroupBy' internal/syntax/parser.go
  (none)

So five kinds were accepted and only two were expressible. The author was told
what was missing, in a file that has no way to supply it.
