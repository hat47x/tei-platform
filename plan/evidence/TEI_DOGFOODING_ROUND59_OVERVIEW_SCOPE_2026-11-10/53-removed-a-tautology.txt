The first version also had:

  TestSectionScopeListsAreDisjoint  (coveredKinds and notCoveredKinds do not
                                     overlap)

A mutation could not break it. sectionScope continues when covered[layer.kind]
is true, so a covered kind has no path into notCoveredKinds. Adding `application`
to the declared-layer table changes nothing.

A test that cannot fail gives false confidence, so I deleted it instead of
counting it as a guard. The three remaining tests each fail under a mutation
recorded in 52.
