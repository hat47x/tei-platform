trace.unresolved          counts ROWS of the selected subject that failed to resolve
trace.projectionUnresolved lists EDGES dropped while building the whole graph

They are different things with similar names. reference/workspace shows
unresolved=0 while projectionUnresolved has 17 entries, so a reader who checks
only the inner counter concludes "nothing unresolved".

Not a defect (the information is present and documented), but a naming collision
worth stating in the contract table for `trace` (#3419).
