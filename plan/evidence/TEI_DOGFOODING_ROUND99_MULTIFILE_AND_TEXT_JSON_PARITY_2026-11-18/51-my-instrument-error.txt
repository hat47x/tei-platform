I first read "text 2 lines, json 5 diagnostics" and suspected the two modes
disagreed. They do not; my grep was wrong.

  grep -E "error|warning"      matches either word anywhere in a line, not only
                                at the beginning. It is not a diagnostic counter.
  Some diagnostic lines        do not contain either literal word in the
                                rendered text, so this grep omits them.

Reading the raw tab-separated output:

  [$1] [warning] HEADER_DETAIL_BINDING_COVERAGE_UNDECLARED
  [$1] [error]   UNKNOWN_SEMANTIC_ATTRIBUTE_TYPE
  [$1] [APPLICATION_RELEASE_CONTRACT_NOT_CURRENT] ...
  [$1] [DATABASE_RELEASE_CONTRACT_NOT_CURRENT] ... (2)

  5 text lines / 5 json diagnostics -> they agree.

Both modes call operations.ValidateWorkspace and share
WorkspaceValidationReportFrom (#3420), so a count mismatch was unlikely by design.
