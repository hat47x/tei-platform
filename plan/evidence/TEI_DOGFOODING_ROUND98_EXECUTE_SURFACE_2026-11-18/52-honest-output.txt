The successful run prints its own limits next to its claim:

  mode: reference (no durable effects)
  committed: true (reference unit of work)
  persisted: false (persistence: not-applicable, durability: none)
  difference-check: not observed (this executor does not enumerate its operations)

JSON also carries appliedUnobserved: true and authorizationMode:
reference-allow-all.

"committed: true" alone could be misread as durable; the same output refuses
that reading on the next line. Not a defect.
