  INVALID_PLUGIN_CAPABILITY_KIND    deployment-executor, observation-provider,

                                    editor-intelligence
  INVALID_PLUGIN_EXTERNAL_SCOPE     editor, build, test, runtime, migration
  INVALID_PLUGIN_EXTERNAL_KIND      toolchain, runtime, language-package, tool

All five were reached through the CLI against a workspace copy. Unlike
INVALID_SEMANTIC_TYPE_KIND (round 54), none of these is unreachable.
