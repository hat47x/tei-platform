This guard covers one step of the chain:

  ir.World --[checked here]--> index.Build --[NOT checked]--> reference graph

A field marked "read" can still lose its substructure on the way. Persistence is
exactly that case: index.Build reads w.Persistence, but PersistenceIndexEntry
drops Mapping.Columns, so column mappings never become edges (#4001). This test
passes while that gap remains.

Closing the second step needs a per-entry-type judgement (which nested fields
matter), so it is left as a separate design question rather than folded in here.
