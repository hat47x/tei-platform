# External Design Reference Note

This path is retained temporarily for existing links.

TEI may consult public material from multiple business-system design and model-driven development approaches when a design question benefits from external comparison. Those references are inputs to inquiry, not semantic authority, compatibility commitments, project lineage, or product evaluation.

TEI design decisions must be explainable from TEI Product Requirements, Architecture, Reference results, ADRs, and Evidence.

For public documentation, keep third-party discussion brief and factual. Do not characterize active software as obsolete or superseded, do not infer author intent, and do not use comparative language unless it is necessary and directly supported.
