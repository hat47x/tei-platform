test(cli): measure which flags l2b.claimed actually needs after §4.30

§7 said l2b.claimed requires the caller to name both the in-process route and
-rebuild-check. §4.30 made the reproduction check default whenever -build-catalog
is given, so the second half of that sentence no longer holds, but nothing had
checked.

Two tests measure it instead of repeating the premise. With only -build-catalog,
the reproducibility record reaches "reproduced" and the claim holds with an empty
unmet list: no -rebuild-check was passed. With an explicit -rebuild-check=false,
the second build does not run, the reason names the caller's choice rather than a
missing flag, and the claim does not hold. Those two cases are distinguished by
the reason text, which is what makes the refusal attributable.

The record also states what did not change: whether a default attempt - one that
names neither route - should be able to claim is still open, and it depends on
§4.29's decision about where a catalog is written. §4.31 corrects the §7 wording
and says plainly that it decides nothing.

tools/README.md does not list tools/reference-tei-eki-contract. That gap predates
this branch, this change does not touch that file, and every Markdown link in the
edited documents resolves.
